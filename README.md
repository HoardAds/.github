# HoardAds

HoardAds is a hand-reviewed directory of AI & crypto tools. Every listing is checked by a human before it goes in, and every paid placement is labeled as paid — in plain sight.

## Explore

- **Directory & reviews** — [hoardads.com](https://hoardads.com)
- **Free Resources Center** — [hoardads.com/free-resources](https://hoardads.com/free-resources/) — hand-verified free tiers, startup credits and genuinely free tools, with the limits and eligibility spelled out for every offer.
- **Awesome AI & Crypto Tools** — [github.com/HoardAds/awesome-ai-crypto-tools](https://github.com/HoardAds/awesome-ai-crypto-tools) — the curated AI & crypto list, updated daily.
- **Awesome Free AI Resources** — [github.com/HoardAds/awesome-free-ai-resources](https://github.com/HoardAds/awesome-free-ai-resources) — free tiers, startup credits and genuinely free tools, checked against the providers' official pages.
